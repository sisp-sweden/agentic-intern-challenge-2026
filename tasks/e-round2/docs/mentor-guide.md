# Mentor guide

Thank you for giving your time to the cohort.

## What we ask

- Up to your monthly capacity in sessions, 45 minutes each.
- Prepare by reading the one-page summary the startup sends the day before.
- Send the startup three bullet points of follow-up within two working days.

## How sessions are booked

Startups list up to three preferred mentors. The coordinator assigns startups to mentors in the order requests arrive, using each mentor's first preference that still has room. Your capacity and the weekdays you are free are kept in `src/mentors/mentors.json`.

## If something goes wrong

If a startup does not show up, tell the coordinator. We would rather rebook than have you wait.
