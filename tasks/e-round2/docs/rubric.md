# Application scoring rubric

This is how Fieldnote scores applications for the accelerator. It applies to round 2 and later calls.

## Criteria

Each criterion is a whole number from 0 to 100, worked out from the application as follows.

| Criterion | How it is scored |
|---|---|
| Team | 10 points per founder, counting at most 4 founders, plus 5 points per year of founder experience, counting at most 12 years |
| Traction | 1 point per 1,000 SEK of monthly recurring revenue, at most 100 |
| Market | 1 point per 50 MSEK of addressable market, at most 100 |
| Product | 1 point per 20 active users, at most 100 |
| Deeptech | 1 point per 25 kSEK of R&D spend, at most 100 |

Fractions are dropped, not rounded. The cap of 4 founders means a team of five or more scores the same on team size as a team of four.

## Weights

Weights are integer percentages and add up to 100.

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Team | 20 |
| Traction | 15 |
| Market | 20 |
| Product | 20 |
| Deeptech | 25 |

## Total score and cutoff

The total score is the sum of each criterion score times its weight, divided by 100, rounded to one decimal. Ties at the rounding step go up: round half up, so 69.95 becomes 70.0.

Applications with a total of 70.0 or more are invited to interview. There is no other ranking step.

## Eligibility

- One application per startup per calendar year; withdrawn applications don't count.
- Applications must be received by the deadline, 2026-03-01 23:59:59 Stockholm time. Later applications are not assessed.
- Applications are assessed as submitted at the deadline.

## Appendix

Revenue reported by entities in Finland or Estonia is in EUR and is converted at EUR 1 = SEK 11.50 before scoring.
