Frequently asked questions

Who can apply?

Any startup with at least one founder working on it full time. We take companies at any stage, from idea to early revenue.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. We do not take equity and we do not charge a fee.

Can I apply more than once?

Once per startup per calendar year. If you withdraw an application, you may apply again in the same year.

How are applications scored?

Every application is scored by the same published rules, which you can read in the call for the current round.

What happens after I apply?

You get a confirmation email straight away. When the review is done, everyone hears from us by email.

Does everyone get an interview?

Every applicant who advances is interviewed. Interviews are 30 minutes, in person at our office in Stockholm or on video.

When do interviews happen?

A few days after the invitations go out. You choose a slot from the list in your invitation email.

Do you work with startups outside Sweden?

Yes. Several of our startups are based in Finland and Estonia, and we run the programme in English.
