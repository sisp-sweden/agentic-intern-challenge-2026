Round 2 call for applications

Round 2 of the Fieldnote accelerator was open for applications until 2026-03-01 23:59:59 Stockholm time. Applications are now closed and under review.

How we assess

Each application is scored on five criteria, each from 0 to 100. Team counts at most 4 founders and 12 years of experience. The criteria are weighted as follows:

CriterionWeight
Team20%
Traction15%
Market20%
Product20%
Deeptech25%

The total is rounded to one decimal, round half up. Applications with a total of 70.0 or more are invited to interview. The full rules are in our scoring rubric.

Currency

Revenue reported by entities in Finland or Estonia is in EUR and is converted at EUR 1 = SEK 11.50 before scoring.

Eligibility

One application per startup per calendar year; withdrawn applications don't count. Applications are assessed as submitted at the deadline.
