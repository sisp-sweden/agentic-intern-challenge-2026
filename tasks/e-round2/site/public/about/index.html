About Fieldnote

We started in 2023 with eight startups and one rented room. Today the programme runs two cohorts a year and works out of an office near Slussen.

We take no equity. The programme is paid for by a small group of regional funders and by companies that want to meet early teams.

The team

A programme lead, a programme coordinator, and a part-time engineer who looks after the tools that run the application process.

Fieldnote, Stockholm. Questions to programme@fieldnote.example.
