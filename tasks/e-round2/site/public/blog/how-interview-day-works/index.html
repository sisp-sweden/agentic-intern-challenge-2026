How interview day works

Interviews are held at the Fieldnote office near Slussen. You will meet a panel of three: one mentor, one person from the programme team and one outside reader.

Bring a laptop in case you want to show the product, but we will not ask for slides. Expect questions about your last three customer conversations, what you would do with six months of runway and what you want from the mentors.

We send the exact time and room in the invitation. If you cannot make your slot, reply to that email as early as you can and we will do our best to move you.

Fieldnote, Stockholm. Questions to programme@fieldnote.example.
