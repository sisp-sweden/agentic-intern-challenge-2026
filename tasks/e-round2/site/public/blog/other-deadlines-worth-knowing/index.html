Other deadlines worth knowing

Fieldnote is not the only door. A few calls from other organisations that close this spring and suit our kind of team:

  • A national innovation grant for first prototypes, closing on 2026-02-18.
  • A Baltic hardware prize, closing at the end of March.
  • A regional travel grant for founders going to a trade fair abroad, rolling.

Check each organisation's own page for the rules. Applications received after the deadline are usually not read, and we cannot change that for you.
