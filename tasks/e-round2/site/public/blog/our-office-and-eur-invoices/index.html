Notes from the finance desk

Two questions came up from our funders this winter.

First, invoices. Funders in Finland and Estonia are invoiced in EUR, funders in Sweden in SEK. We book everything in SEK at the rate on the invoice date.

Second, reporting. We publish a short annual report each spring. It lists how many teams we took in, how many finished the programme and what we spent per team. It does not list individual companies unless they ask us to.
