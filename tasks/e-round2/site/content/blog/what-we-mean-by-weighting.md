# What we mean by weighting at demo day

At demo day two groups vote: the audience in the room and the mentor panel. Until this winter every vote counted the same. That made the result depend on how many friends a team brought.

From the winter demo day the mentor panel's votes count double. The audience still decides the audience prize. We will review the weighting after the spring event and tell you what we decided.
