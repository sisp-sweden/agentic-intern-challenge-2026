# Round 1 is done

Invitations for the first round went out this week. Thank you to everyone who applied, and to the mentors who read applications on top of their own jobs.

We read a lot of applications from teams who had clearly never talked to a customer. The ones that stood out had a paragraph with a real name in it: a real clinic, a real warehouse, a real invoice. If you did not make it this time, that is the cheapest thing to fix before round 2.

The call for round 2 opens tomorrow on the programme page.
