# Round 1 call for applications

Round 1 of the Fieldnote accelerator is open for applications until 2026-01-05 23:59:59 Stockholm time.

## How we assess

Each application is scored on five criteria, each from 0 to 100. Team counts at most 4 founders. The criteria are weighted as follows:

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Team | 20% |
| Traction | 25% |
| Market | 20% |
| Product | 20% |
| Deeptech | 15% |

Applications with a total of 70.0 or more are invited to interview.
