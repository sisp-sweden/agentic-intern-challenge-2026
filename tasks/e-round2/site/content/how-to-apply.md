# How to apply

Open calls are listed under [Calls](/calls/). Each call page states its own deadline and how applications are assessed, so read the page for the call you are applying to.

You apply with a short form: who you are, what you have built, who pays for it and how big the market is. You can save a draft and come back to it.

After the deadline the programme team reads the applications and invitations to interview go out by email.
